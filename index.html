About the Conference

The Interuniversity Cardio Genetics Conference brings together researchers, clinicians and experts working in cardiovascular genetics.

This one-day meeting in Leuven provides an opportunity to exchange knowledge, discuss current developments in cardiogenetics and connect researchers and clinicians from different universities and institutions.
