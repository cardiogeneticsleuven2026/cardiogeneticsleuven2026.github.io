About the Conference

A one-day scientific meeting bringing together clinical, genetic and experimental perspectives in cardiovascular genetics.

The Interuniversity Cardio Genetics Conference brings together researchers, clinicians and experts working in cardiovascular genetics.

The conference is organised around three complementary perspectives: clinical genetics and cardiology, genetic research, and model systems and development. The meeting provides an opportunity to exchange knowledge, discuss current developments in cardiogenetics and connect researchers and clinicians from different universities and institutions.

Scientific programme

Confirmed Speakers

Three panels spanning clinical cardiogenetics, genetic research and experimental model systems.

JD
Panel 1 · Clinical genetics and cardiology

Prof. Dr. Julie De Backer

Ghent University & UZ Gent

  • Centre for Medical Genetics

Prof. Dr. Julie De Backer is a cardiologist and clinical geneticist at Ghent University Hospital and professor at Ghent University. Her clinical and research interests focus on Heritable Thoracic Aortic Diseases (HTAD), including Marfan syndrome and related disorders, as well as Adult Congenital Heart Disease (ACHD). Her translational research combines clinical studies with genetically engineered animal models to identify novel therapeutic targets and improve the diagnosis, treatment, and long-term management of patients with inherited cardiovascular diseases. She leads and participates in several international multicentre studies on topics such as pregnancy and ageing in congenital heart disease and plays an active role in European guideline development, having co-chaired the 2020 ESC Guidelines on Adult Congenital Heart Disease and the 2025 ESC Guidelines on Cardiovascular Disease during Pregnancy.

Panel 2 · Genetic research

Prof. Dr. Lars Allan Larsen

University of Copenhagen

  • Faculty of Health and Medical Sciences
  • Department of Cellular and Molecular Medicine

Prof. Dr. Lars Allan Larsen is professor of Developmental Genetics at the University of Copenhagen. His research focuses on the fundamental genetic and molecular mechanisms underlying human embryonic development and congenital disorders, with a particular interest in cardiac development and congenital heart disease (CHD). His group combines human genetic studies with functional approaches in cellular models, tissues, and zebrafish to uncover novel disease genes and regulatory mechanisms involved in embryonic development. By integrating genetic discovery with functional validation, his research aims to contribute to a better understanding of the molecular basis of congenital heart defects and other developmental disorders.

Panel 3 · Model systems and development

Dr. Sonia Stefanovic

Aix-Marseille University

  • French Institute of Health and Medical Research
  • Centre for CardioVascular and Nutrition Research (C2VN, Marseille)

Dr. Sonia Stefanovic is a group leader at the Centre for CardioVascular and Nutrition Research at Aix-Marseille University. Her research focuses on the molecular mechanisms underlying cardiac development and congenital heart disease, with a particular interest in how genetic, metabolic and environmental factors interact during embryonic heart formation. Her group combines mouse models, pluripotent stem cell-derived cardiac organoids, and state-of-the-art transcriptomic, epigenomic and single-cell multi-omics approaches to investigate gene regulatory networks and epigenetic mechanisms. Current research focuses on the impact of maternal pregestational diabetes on fetal heart development and the role of gene–environment interactions in congenital heart defects.
