Panel 1 · Clinical genetics and cardiology

Prof. Dr. Julie De Backer

Ghent University Hospital & Ghent University

  • Department of Cardiology and Centre for Medical Genetics
  • Faculty of Medicine and Health Sciences – Department of Internal Medicine and Pediatrics

Prof. Dr. Julie De Backer is a cardiologist and clinical geneticist at Ghent University Hospital and professor at Ghent University. Her clinical and research interests focus on Heritable Thoracic Aortic Diseases (HTAD), including Marfan syndrome and related disorders, as well as Adult Congenital Heart Disease (ACHD). Her translational research combines clinical studies with genetically engineered animal models to identify novel therapeutic targets and improve the diagnosis, treatment, and long-term management of patients with inherited cardiovascular diseases. She leads and participates in several international multicentre studies and networks on topics such as pregnancy and ageing in congenital heart disease and played an active role in European Society for Cardiology guideline development, having co-chaired the 2020 ESC Guidelines on Adult Congenital Heart Disease and the 2025 ESC Guidelines on Cardiovascular Disease during Pregnancy.
